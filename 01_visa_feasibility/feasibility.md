# Phase 1: Visa & feasibility check: 6-month PFE for a Tunisian student, start Jan or Feb 2027

**All sources checked on 2026-11-03.** This is a summary, not legal advice. Anything marked **UNVERIFIED** comes from a secondary source or could not be confirmed on an official page.

**Your constraints:** you are a Tunisian national enrolled at ESPRIT. You need a 6-month mandatory PFE starting 2027-01-01 (ideal) or 2027-02-01 (latest), finishing before September 2027. That means a start no later than about **2027-03-01**, and only if ESPRIT accepts a March start (**confirm with ESPRIT**).

---

## TL;DR

1. **France is the most realistic country abroad.** The process is short and standard: a tripartite *convention de stage*, then a long-stay "stagiaire" visa through TLScontact Tunis. A **Feb 2027** start is realistic if the convention is signed by about mid-December 2026. A Jan 1 start is very tight.
2. **Germany is possible but slow.** The host company must first get approval from the Federal Employment Agency (BA/ZAV). After that you can book TLScontact directly (no 12-month waitlist), but the embassy may take **up to 3 months** to decide. Jan 2027 is **not realistic**. Feb is borderline. **March 2027** is the realistic target, so you need an offer by early December 2026 at the latest.
3. **Switzerland is narrow.** Only *mandatory* university internships qualify. Zurich wants applications to go through IAESTE or AIESEC, the host company needs approval from the canton plus SEM, and you must be paid a customary Swiss salary. Apply only to big groups that already host non-EU interns.
4. **USA (J-1 intern) is possible on paper** but needs a willing host plus a paid sponsor. **Canada, UAE and Qatar are near-impossible** for a 6-month Jan/Feb 2027 PFE.
5. **Tunisia needs no visa** and is your safety net. Run it in parallel from day 1, targeting reputable groups and AI companies in Tunis.

**Where to spend effort:** about 40% France, 20% Germany, 25% Tunisia, 10% Switzerland, 5% USA. Canada, UAE and Qatar get nothing except opportunistic leads.

## What this changes

- **Use Feb 1 (France) or Mar 1 (Germany/Switzerland) as working start dates**, and write "start **Feb–Mar 2027**, 6 months" on the CV and in letters. This needs ESPRIT to accept a March start.
- **Germany:** in every application, say you are enrolled at a foreign university, the internship is **mandatory**, and it needs **BA approval under § 15 Nr. 6 BeschV**, filed by the host. Prefer companies with formal international-intern programs (Bosch, Siemens, SAP, Airbus…).
- **France:** emphasise "convention de stage tripartite with ESPRIT ready" and "gratification per French law". Most large French groups already handle Tunisian PFE interns routinely.
- **Tunisia:** apply in parallel now. Reputable AI and data teams in Tunis (and Tunisian subsidiaries of EU groups) can give you an international environment without a visa.
- **Post-graduation (2027+):** the Swiss Young Professionals permit (Tunisia is a partner, up to 18 months) and Germany's job-seeker or skilled-worker routes open up once you hold the degree.

## Confirm with ESPRIT
1. Is a PFE start in **March 2027** accepted (deadline for the defence)?
2. Can ESPRIT sign a **tripartite convention** quickly, including in English for German or Swiss hosts?
3. Can ESPRIT issue an **English/German letter** stating "the internship is a mandatory part of the curriculum" (needed for Germany and Switzerland)?
4. Is ESPRIT connected to **IAESTE Tunisia** for Swiss placements?

## Confirm with embassies / authorities
- Germany: the current ZAV processing time and whether "Praktikum im Einvernehmen" TLS slots are actually available (check the TLScontact Tunis site yourself; it blocked automated access).
- France: whether the convention must be *visée* by the préfecture/DREETS for Tunisian nationals in 2027, plus current TLS appointment availability.
