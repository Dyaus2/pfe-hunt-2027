# Application plan: 2026-11-05 → 2026-12-31

**Logic** (from `01_visa_feasibility/feasibility.md`):
- **France first.** It is the fastest visa route, and many live 2027 offers exist now (Thales, Airbus/ATR, Safran, Sanofi).
- **Germany and Switzerland are front-loaded.** The host needs BA approval or a canton/SEM permit, then the visa, so offers must land by **early December** to start in **March**.
- **Tunisia runs in parallel** as a safety net. Most Tunisian PFE books are published Nov–Dec.

**Volume target:** about 60–70 applications in 8 weeks, at least 70% of them on live offers.
**Tracking:** fill the `status` column in `targets.xlsx` (dropdown: to apply / applied / followed-up / interview / offer / rejected / skip).

## Daily rhythm (about 2 hours a day)
1. **15 min:** re-scan live boards (Thales/Airbus/Sanofi Workday, Bosch SmartRecruiters, Welcome to the Jungle, Keejob) for new "2027" offers.
2. **60 min:** two applications. Adapt the master letter: first paragraph (posting-specific) and one proof point.
3. **15 min:** follow-ups due today (see rules below).
4. **30 min:** update `targets.xlsx`; LinkedIn outreach to one team member (not HR) per A/B target, using a short note (template in `04_letters/`).

## Follow-up rules
- **Day 7** after applying: follow-up #1 (template `04_letters/followup_email_EN_FR.md`), only where you have an email or LinkedIn contact.
- **Day 14:** follow-up #2, a shorter message that adds one new proof point (e.g. a link to a Phase 4 PFE-proposal repo).
- After that, stop. Mark the row "rejected" (silent) and move on.
- **Rejection:** reply with a thank-you and ask whether another team runs a PFE in 2027 (often yields a referral).

## Visa clock: what to do the day you get an offer
- **France:** ask HR to start the *convention de stage* at once, and send them ESPRIT's convention contact. Book a TLScontact Tunis appointment **the same day** for a date about 3 months before the start (France-Visas allows lodging up to 3 months early).
- **Germany:** send the employer the BA form link (*Erfassungsbogen*: https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/Erfassung-Praktika_ba015613.pdf) and ask them to file with the ZAV immediately. Then book TLS "Praktikum im Einvernehmen". Get German translations of the ESPRIT documents started (needed for the embassy).
- **Switzerland:** get ESPRIT's English letter confirming the internship is **mandatory**. The employer files the permit with the canton.
